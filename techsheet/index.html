CP-300

System overview

How it works

Sand sits on a 200 × 200 mm aluminium plate under an acrylic window. Holding a pad plays a tone through a transducer under the plate. At resonance, sand moves off the vibrating areas and collects on the still lines, forming a Chladni figure.

Tones are generated on the ESP32. No computer or internet on site. Tuning is done from a phone over the controller's own Wi-Fi.

Behaviour

  • Hold to play, release to mute. Max 5 s per press.
  • One pad at a time; first pad held wins.
  • Boots muted.
  • 20–4000 Hz, 0.1 Hz steps. Presets kept after power off.

Page won't open

  • "No internet" warning: stay connected.
  • Turn off mobile data.
  • One phone at a time.

Tuning

  1. Power on. Unit boots muted.
  2. Join the Wi-Fi, open http://192.168.4.1, tap Hubungkan kontrol.
  3. Pour 5 g of sand.
  4. Set FREQ, LEVEL, FOLD, WAVE, HARMONICS. Press TEST SUARA to hear it on the plate.
  5. Press SIMPAN 1–4 to save to a pad.
  6. Press Selesai servis when done. Pads work without the phone.

MUTE / STOP is always on screen. If the phone disconnects, the unit mutes after 2.5 s.

Site requirements and operation

Site requirements

FootprintØ500 mm
SurfaceLevel, stable
Power1 outlet, 5 V adaptor
AirflowAway from AC vents and fans
Wi-Fi2.4 GHz. One SSID per unit
InternetNot needed

Daily

  1. Plug in. Unit boots muted.
  2. Pour 5 g of sand.
  3. Test each pad.
  4. Run GATHER when sand drifts to the edge.
  5. Top up 5 g when figures fade.
  6. Unplug at close. Wipe the acrylic.
